Frequently asked questions

How do I get Twine?

Only as a participant in the study. You receive an invitation for Apple's "TestFlight" app and install Twine through it. Twine is not available on the App Store.

What do I need?

Your own iPhone with the TestFlight app and the sign-in details you receive from the study lead. Your partner also needs their own iPhone and their own sign-in details.

What is the recovery key for?

It is your only spare key for chat, journal and milestones. On a new iPhone or after reinstalling, you enter it to restore this data. Without it, earlier content can no longer be read on the device – not by the study lead either.

Keep it in a password manager only you open, or on paper in a place only you can reach. Better not as a photo, in shared notes or in a chat.

I don't get any notifications.

Check the "Notification windows" in Twine: if they are turned on, notifications only appear within your time windows. Also check in the iOS settings that notifications are allowed for Twine.

How do I report a problem?

Write to us what happened and roughly when. Twine also has a "Diagnostic log": a summary of technical findings that you can review and share yourself. It stays on your device unless you share it and contains no messages, names or identifiers.

Feedback sent through TestFlight goes to the developer and also to Apple.

What are crash reports?

An optional switch in the settings, off by default. What a report contains and where it goes is described in the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

Write to us. What happens on the server and where the limits are is described under Retention and deletion.

Where can I find the source code?

Twine is free software under the GNU AGPL-3.0 and uses, among other things, the Matrix SDK. You can get the complete source code of the version you use from the developer free of charge on request – for at least three years. Write to support@devsalah.com.

Twine – research prototype, not on the App Store.
